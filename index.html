About this data

Every number on this map comes from real weather stations, not a climate model: raw hourly observations from NOAA's public Integrated Surface Database (ISD), 2010–2025, processed directly into the metrics shown here (perfect/miserable days, streaks, vegetation, and so on).

Station instrumentation and reporting frequency vary a lot — some report every hour, others every few hours, and coverage gaps are common. Click any station and open "Data details" to see exactly how much data it has, which years counted, and why.
